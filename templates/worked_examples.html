How to read these examples

Candidate retrieval

RNAdex stores nucleotide graphs with backbone, Watson-Crick, and generic non-Watson-Crick edges. These features efficiently narrow the structural search space.

Coordinate validation

A generic graph edge does not preserve every interaction family or geometric detail. Each named-motif page therefore states its separate acceptance rule and validation evidence.
